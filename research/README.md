# STARWEFT research archive

This directory preserves evidence used to develop [the product contract](../SPEC.md).
It is not a separate design authority. Product decisions, progress, and acceptance
criteria live only in `SPEC.md`.

## Supplied roguelike corpus

The original seven-source corpus is preserved without editorial changes under
`sources/roguelike/`:

- `Analysis The Eight Rules Of Roguelike Design.md`
- `CARMSA-12 - roguelike peer reviewed philosophy.pdf`
- `Game Studies - Genre, Prototype Theory and the Berlin Interpretation of Roguelikes.md`
- `Quasimorph Research Paper 2026.pdf`
- `What Makes A Good Roguelike.md`
- `What Makes a Roguelike Good. It feels like roguelikes and roguelites... by Shawn Medium.md`
- `What makes a roguelike good rroguelikes (1).md`

Some filenames contain typographic punctuation in the original filesystem; use
`rg --files research/sources/roguelike` to obtain exact paths.

Section 28 of `SPEC.md` records the synthesis, primary comparative sources, and the
trace from research lessons to product requirements. Raw sources remain here so
future revisions can re-check the evidence rather than copying another summary into
the live documentation set.
